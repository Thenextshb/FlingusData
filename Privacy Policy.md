# Flingus Privacy Policy

Flingus collects only what it needs to run online play, keep your Scraps and cosmetics, and keep players safe. We never sell your data.

This policy covers the Flingus VR game ("Flingus", "we", "us") and the services it uses to work. By playing Flingus, you agree to this policy.

## What we collect

We do not ask for your real name, email, age, or payment details. The game collects the following.

| What | Why |
| --- | --- |
| A device ID made by the game | Signs you in to your Flingus account without a password |
| Your player ID | Identifies your account across sessions |
| Your in-game name | Shown above your monke to other players |
| Your monke color and the cosmetics you wear | Shown to other players |
| Your Scraps balance and cosmetics you own | Saved so you keep them between sessions |
| Which rooms you join, and when | Online play, moderation and anti-cheat |
| Reports you make or that are made about you | Moderation |
| Moderator actions on your account (warnings, bans, gifts) | Moderation |
| Information about your copy of the game, such as whether it has been modified | Anti-cheat |
| Your voice, while you are in a room | Live voice chat with nearby players |

The game may also save a log file on your headset to help find bugs. It stays on your headset unless you send it to us.

## How we use it

We use your information only to run and protect the game:

- Sign you in and save your progress, Scraps and cosmetics
- Put you in rooms and show you to other players
- Give daily rewards and gifts
- Look into reports, and warn or ban players who break the rules
- Detect cheating and modified copies of the game
- Fix bugs

We do not use your data for ads, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

## Services we use

Flingus relies on these companies to work. Each handles data under its own privacy policy.

| Service | What it does | What it gets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [PlayFab (Microsoft)](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement) | Accounts, saved data, Scraps, cosmetics, bans | Device ID, player ID, name, saved game data |
| [Photon (Exit Games)](https://www.photonengine.com/privacy) | Multiplayer rooms and voice chat | Player ID, name, room, voice audio, IP address |
| [Meta](https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/) | The Quest platform and store | Whatever Meta collects under its own policy |
| [LIV](https://www.liv.tv/privacy) | Optional in-game camera for recording | Only if you choose to use it |

## Voice chat

Voice chat is live and is not recorded or saved by us. Your voice is sent through Photon to the other players in your room while you are in it.

Other players can hear you, and they may record the game themselves. You can turn off your microphone for Flingus in your headset's settings.

## Moderation and anti-cheat

To keep Flingus fair and safe, our moderators can see your player name, player ID, the rooms you join, reports about you, and anti-cheat alerts. These are posted to private moderation channels only our team can see.

Players who break the rules can be warned or banned. Using a modified copy of the game can get your account banned permanently.

## Kids

Flingus is for players 13 and older. If you are under 13, do not play Flingus. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and any account we find that belongs to a child under 13 will be banned and its data deleted.

## Keeping your data

We keep your account data while you play Flingus. Moderation records, such as bans, are kept as long as needed to keep the game safe.
